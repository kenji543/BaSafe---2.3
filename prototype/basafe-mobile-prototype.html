Info & Limitations

Basafe is a preliminary screening tool. Read the limitations before making a decision.
What the system does not do

It does not forecast disasters, monitor emergencies in real time, inspect a site, evaluate a structure, or declare a location safe or unsafe.

About Basafe

A public Web-GIS research prototype that makes selected hazard evidence, model reasoning, uncertainty, and limitations easier to understand for Basey, Samar.

Privacy & damage reports

The Report Damage form collects your pinned location, damage details, optional photos, and your name and phone number, only after you agree. These are used by Basey MDRRMO responders to find you and respond, and are never shown publicly.

Required disclaimer

This report is a preliminary multi-hazard screening output based on selected available data. It does not certify that a location is safe or unsafe and does not replace official hazard, planning, engineering, geological, geotechnical, or regulatory assessment.
